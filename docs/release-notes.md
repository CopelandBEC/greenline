Greenline 2.1.0-rc.1 — fast PDF markup for drawing, spec and deck review. A release candidate: please try it and report anything off before 2.1.0.

**Install (Windows 10/11, 64-bit):** download `Greenline-2.1.0-rc.1-setup.exe` below and run it. No admin rights needed; it installs for you only. Installing over Redline or an earlier Greenline keeps your markups.
Because the installer isn't code-signed yet, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info → Run anyway**.

**New in 2.1**
- **Note boxes take a height.** Drag a note's or callout's bottom edge or corner (or draw the box with `T`); it keeps that size. **Fit text to box** (the ⏷ beside the text size) sizes the text to the biggest that fits it.
- **Text alignment.** Left, centre or right, and top, middle or bottom, from the menu beside **B I U**.
- **Text sizes from 4 to 72 pt.** Type or drag any size in the toolbar; presets in its ⏷ menu.
- **Arrange.** Line selected markups up by their left, centre, right, top, middle or bottom edges (one on its own, or one group, lines up with the page), or **centre them on the page**.
- **Shift keeps it straight.** Lines, arrows, measurements, a line's ends and moves go across, up and down, or at 45°.
- **Copy and paste markups.** `Ctrl+C` / `Ctrl+X` / `Ctrl+V`, pasted where the pointer is, also into another tab. Text copied from another program pastes as a note.
- **Open a page in a new tab or split view.** Right-click a page in the thumbnails or overview. A new tab is another view of the same document, sharing its markups; tabs on one document show their page.
- **Exports say they're done.** A notice in the corner names the file, with **Open** and **Show in folder** (PDF exports, smaller copies, comment and measurement lists). A failed export stays up until closed.
- **`Shift+I` inserts an image.**
- A note placed with a click (`T`) starts its text at the click, not below it.
- Notes running off the edge of a page still show past it on screen.
- For agents: `callout` and `text` ops take `box_height`, `fit`, `align` and `valign`.

Press `F1` in the app for all shortcuts, your name on exported comments, appearance, and the pattern library folder.
